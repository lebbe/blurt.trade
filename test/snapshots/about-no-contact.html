About blurt.trade

A small hobby site for blurting.

blurt.trade is a small hobby site for short posts, blurts, of up to 280 characters. It has no ads and no tracking, and collects as little about you as it can: see Privacy and Terms. It works fully without JavaScript, which is optional.

JavaScript

Everything works without JavaScript. The site's JavaScript is optional, and comes only from blurt.trade itself. It is off for your account: turn it on in Settings.
