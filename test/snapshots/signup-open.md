# Create a profile

[Back to blurt.trade](</>)

Send `POST /signup` as a form or as JSON with `username` (letters, numbers and `_`), `password` (at least 8 characters) and, optionally, `remember` (`1` to stay logged in; otherwise the login ends when the browser closes, or after a day unused).

Keep your password safe, e.g. in a password manager. We don't take an email address, so there's no password reset: a lost password can't be recovered, and neither can the account.
